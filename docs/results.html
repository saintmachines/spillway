FAQ · adding it to an agent

Before you add it to your agent.

Does it need a model?

No. The sanitizer and the floor are rules. The judge is optional and works with an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, Guard(task, judge=Judge(endpoint, model)). Without a judge, a call only the judge could admit is refused. The paper's Table 3 disables recovery of refused identity targets while keeping fixed delegation rules and the object judge enabled. The qwen3:8b cells have zero attack success, at mean utility .354 instead of .370.

How large must the judge be?

The paper uses a 4-bit gemma4:31b on local Ollama. A 4-bit gemma4:12b also kept zero attack success on the qwen3:8b cells and the ChatInject panel, at utility .348 instead of .370.

My tools are not AgentDojo's. What do I declare?

Return typed objects, such as pydantic models. Declare your tool-name prefixes and target-argument names once, for example Lexicon().extend(target_arg_hints={"payee"}). Then pass the lexicon to Guard.

What happens when a call is refused?

The agent gets a tool message, decision.refusal_text(name), that asks it to retry with values from the user's request. The episode goes on.

SPILLWAY checks recipients against the user's task and the typed fields of tool returns.1
