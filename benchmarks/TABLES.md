# SPILLWAY benchmark tables

Generated by `benchmarks/scripts/make_tables.py` from `benchmarks/results/*.json` (the per-cell results released with the paper). Every printed value is read or computed from those files and compared with the paper's printed numbers, except that ROPE's two ChatInject composites use the benign utility the paper prints. Result: 590 checks, 530 printed values against the paper and 60 consistency checks on the files, 0 mismatches.

Conventions: BU = benign utility, UAU = utility under attack, ASR = in-scope attack success (executed effect, content-manipulation tasks dropped), NRP = BU x (1 - ASR), printed NRP (BU) in Table 2 beside NRP (UAU) = UAU x (1 - ASR), ERS = harmonic mean of (1 - ASR) and BU. Higher is better except ASR. ASR .000 means no in-scope hit on these AgentDojo cells; the guarantee is scoped to identity targets, a spoofed typed field is out of scope.
